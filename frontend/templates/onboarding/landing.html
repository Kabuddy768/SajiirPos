Platform Features

Everything you need to run your store

From the till to the boardroom — Sajiir gives every team member exactly what they need.

🛒

Offline-First POS Till

Keep selling even when the internet goes down. Transactions sync automatically when connectivity is restored — no lost sales, ever.

📦

Smart Inventory Management

Track stock across multiple branches, set reorder points, manage batch/expiry dates, and get automated low-stock alerts via WhatsApp.

🏭

Supplier & Purchasing

Record Goods Received Notes, manage credit vs. cash purchases, track payables aging, and reorder directly from the supplier dashboard.

🧾

KRA eTIMS Compliance

Every invoice is automatically signed and submitted to KRA in the background. Track submission status, retry failures, and audit your eTIMS history.

📊

Profit & Loss Reporting

See your revenue, cost of sales, gross margin, and net profit for any period. Break down expenses by category to find where money is going.

👥

Multi-Branch Staff Management

Invite staff by email, assign roles (Owner, Admin, Manager, Cashier, Auditor), and control access per branch. Monitor performance and flag anomalies.

💳

Customer Loyalty & Credit

Build repeat business with a points-based loyalty program. Offer credit sales to trusted customers with configurable limits and aging reports.

↩️

Returns & Refunds

Process full or partial returns against any sale. Inventory is automatically restocked. Refunds above KES 5,000 require manager approval.

📱

WhatsApp Receipts & Reorders

Send customer receipts via WhatsApp in one click. Auto-compose reorder messages directly to suppliers when stock hits the reorder threshold.
